ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A legacy of learning since 1975.

Gyanodaya Bal Batika School is a school in Bungmati / Khokana, Lalitpur, Nepal.

The school was established in 1975 A.D. by Mrs. Indira Yakthumba in fond memory of her husband, His Excellency Late Gyan Bahadur Yakthumba.

Our Journey Decades of education, memories and milestones continue to shape our school community.
