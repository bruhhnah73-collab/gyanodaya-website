BEYOND STUDIES

Learning goes beyond the classroom.

At Gyanodaya Bal Batika, students are encouraged to explore their interests, develop new skills and take part in activities beyond their regular studies.

⚽

Sports

Students participate in sports and physical activities that encourage teamwork, discipline and a healthy lifestyle.

🎨

Creativity

Art, creativity and cultural activities give students opportunities to express themselves and discover their talents.

💻

Technology

Students explore technology and digital skills to prepare themselves for an increasingly connected world.

🤝

Community

School activities help students build confidence, leadership skills and strong relationships with their community.

A school is more than a building.
