WHO WE ARE

A school with a meaningful history.

Gyanodaya Bal Batika School is located in Bungmati / Khokana, Lalitpur, Nepal.

The school was established in 1975 A.D. by Mrs. Indira Yakthumba in fond memory of her husband, His Excellency Late Gyan Bahadur Yakthumba.

Over the years, the school has continued its journey of education and has become part of the community it serves.
